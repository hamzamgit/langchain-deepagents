## Refund Policy

### Eligibility
- Duplicate charges for the same subscription on the same day
- Accidental upgrades within 48 hours
- Service outages lasting more than 24 consecutive hours (pro-rated credit)

### Timeline
Approved refunds typically appear on your statement within **5–10 business days**.

### Process
1. Support investigates transactions in our billing system.
2. A refund request is prepared.
3. A human reviewer must approve sensitive refunds.
4. Once approved, the refund is recorded and you receive confirmation.

### Not eligible
- Charges older than 90 days
- Partial months after voluntary cancellation (unless required by law)
- Fees already refunded

We never process refunds without verification. Agents must not claim a refund is complete until the system records it.
