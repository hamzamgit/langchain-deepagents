## Password Reset

### How to reset
1. Go to the login page and select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email associated with your account.
3. Open the reset link sent to your email (valid for 60 minutes).
4. Choose a new password (minimum 12 characters).

### Support-assisted reset
If you cannot access your email, contact support. An agent can initiate a password reset request for the email on file. For security, we never send the new password in chat.

### Tips
- Check spam/junk folders for the reset email.
- Ensure you use the same email registered on the account.
- After resetting, sign out of other devices from Account → Security.
