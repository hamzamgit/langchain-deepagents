## Billing & Subscriptions

### Plans
- **Basic**: $9.99/month — core features
- **Pro**: $29.99/month — advanced features and priority email support
- **Enterprise**: $99/month — dedicated support and SSO

### Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal.

### Invoices
Invoices are emailed within 24 hours of a successful charge. You can also request a copy from support.

### Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry up to 3 times over 7 days. Update your payment method in Account → Billing to avoid service interruption.

### Duplicate charges
If you see two identical successful charges for the same subscription on the same day, contact support. Verified duplicates are eligible for a refund of the extra charge after human review.
