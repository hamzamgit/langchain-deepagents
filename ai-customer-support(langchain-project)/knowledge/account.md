## Account Management

### Account status
Accounts may be: **active**, **suspended**, or **closed**.
Suspended accounts cannot access paid features until the issue is resolved.

### Updating profile information
You can update your display name in Account → Profile.
Changing the account email requires identity verification and human approval.

### Login issues
If you cannot log in:
1. Confirm you are using the correct email.
2. Try a password reset.
3. Check whether the account is suspended.

### Account deletion
Account deletion is permanent and requires human approval. Contact support to request deletion.
